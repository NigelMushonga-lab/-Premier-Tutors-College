About Us

We are the world's finest private tutors. Premier Tutors (PT) offers personalized education for Grade 6 to Form 6 with highly qualified tutors and small class sizes that ensure individual attention. Our 1:1 and small-group tutoring embraces 21st-century pedagogy, including flipped learning and differentiated instruction.

Mission & Vision

Vision

We offer broad-based instruction with small class sizes and personalized attention. Subjects include:
